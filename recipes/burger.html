Ingredients:

  • Warm water: 1 1/3 cups (105°F to 115°F)
  • Yeast: 2 1/4 teaspoons (1 active dry or instant yeast packet)
  • Sugar: 1 to 2 teaspoons (helps activate the yeast)
  • Flour: 3 1/2 to 4 cups all-purpose flour or bread flour
  • Olive oil: 2 tablespoons (extra-virgin)
  • Salt: 1 teaspoon
  • Cornmeal: Optional, for dusting the pan or pizza stone so the dough doesn't stick
  • Canned crushed tomatoes or tomato puree: 14 oz (400g)
  • Garlic: 1 to 2 cloves, minced
  • Olive oil: 1 to 2 tablespoons
  • Dried herbs: 1 teaspoon oregano or basil
  • Salt and sugar: 1/4 teaspoon of each (to taste)
  • Cheese: 1.5 to 2 cups shredded low-moisture mozzarella cheese
  • Proteins: Sliced pepperoni, cooked Italian sausage, or ham
  • Veggies: Thinly sliced bell peppers, mushrooms, onions, or black olives

Steps:

  1. Mix warm water, sugar, and yeast in a bowl. Wait 5 minutes until bubbly.
  2. Add flour, salt, and olive oil.
  3. Knead the dough for 5 minutes until smooth.
  4. Cover the dough with a clean cloth.
  5. Let it rest in a warm spot for 1 hour until it grows double in size.
  6. Chop or blend tomatoes with a little garlic, salt, sugar, and oregano.
  7. Shred your mozzarella cheese.
  8. Heat your oven hot to 230°C (450°F).
  9. Roll the dough flat into a round circle.
  10. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce on top.
  11. Add the shredded cheese and toppings.
  12. Bake in the oven for 10 to 15 minutes until the crust turns golden and the cheese bubbles
