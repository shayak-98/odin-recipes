Biriyani

Description:

Biryani is a famous mixed rice dish from South Asia made with

fragrant basmati rice, spices, and meat or vegetables. Cooks layer

the ingredients and slow-cook them in a sealed pot so the rich juices soak into the rice.

Ingredients:

  • Long-grain Basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes)
  • Meat (chicken, mutton, or beef) or vegetables / paneer
  • Plain yogurt (curd)
  • Sliced onions and chopped tomatoes
  • Fresh ginger-garlic paste
  • (clarified butter) or cooking oil
  • Saffron soaked in warm milk (optional, for color and aroma

Steps:

  1. Prepare and Marinate the Chicken
  2. Cook the Fried Onions (Barista)
  3. Parboil the Basmati Rice
  4. Cover the dough with a clean cloth.
  5. Cook the Chicken Base
  6. Layer and Dum (Steam) Cook
